Common questions

A worker forgot their password — how do I reset it?

As a manager, open the Manage menu → Workers tab. Find their name and tap Reset password to set a new one for them.

How do I add another manager or worker?

Same place — Manage → Workers tab has separate sections for adding managers and workers. Each person gets their own username and password.

A barcode won't scan — what do I do?

Small or glossy packaging (like candy bars) can be genuinely hard for any phone camera to read. Type the UPC in by hand instead, or check "This item doesn't have a barcode" if there really isn't one printed.

Why does every item need a category now?

Categories power a lot of the reporting (waste by category, filtering the dashboard) — making it required keeps that data actually useful. If you don't have the right one yet, you can create a new category right from the item form.

How does invoice import work?

Upload a photo or PDF of a vendor invoice and it reads each line item into a pending queue for you to review. Nothing is added to your floor stock automatically — you'll fill in or confirm each item's expiration date before it's added, same as completing a regular scan.

What happens when my free trial ends?

You'll get a heads-up a few days before it ends. Add billing under Manage → Billing anytime to keep things running without interruption.

I run more than one store — can they share data?

Each location has its own separate inventory, but if your account has multiple stores, managers can see a combined view across all of them from the "All Locations" dashboard, including transfer suggestions when one location is overstocked on something another is running low on.
