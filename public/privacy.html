2. How we use it

  • To run the Service: storing and displaying your inventory, sending expiration alerts, and managing worker access.
  • To look up product names from a UPC you scan or enter, we send that UPC (not any of your store's other data) to third-party product databases.
  • To send the daily expiration digest email, if enabled, to the manager's email address.
  • To process subscription payments, via Stripe.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use your inventory data for advertising.

4. Data retention

We keep store and inventory data for as long as the account is active. If you close a store account, we'll delete or anonymize its data within [X days], except where we need to keep records for legal or billing reasons.

5. Your choices

  • Managers can remove worker accounts anytime, which also signs that worker out everywhere.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your store's data by contacting us.
  • You can export your inventory data anytime via the CSV export feature in the app.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's privacy

FloorStock is a business tool and isn't directed at or intended for use by children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to managers by email or an in-app notice where practical.
